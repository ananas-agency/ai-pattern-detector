Quillfield Freight Opens Second Ohio Distribution Center

DAYTON, Ohio, April 8, 2026 — Quillfield Freight today opened its second distribution center, a 240,000-square-foot building on the city's east side. The site adds 85 jobs and doubles the company's same-day delivery capacity in western Ohio.

"This opening stands as a testament to our unwavering commitment to the communities we serve," said Mara Ostrander, chief executive of Quillfield Freight. "It's not just a warehouse — it's a gateway to seamless logistics that will empower local businesses to unlock their full potential."

The center runs two shifts and begins taking regional orders on April 15. It uses the same routing software as the company's first site in Columbus.

"Dayton marks a pivotal moment in our journey," Ostrander said. "We're thrilled to embark on this next chapter alongside the partners who made it possible."

Quillfield Freight was founded in 2011 and employs 410 people in Ohio.
